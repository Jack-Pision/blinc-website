## 1. The 2026 Visual Paradigm vs. Outdated 2015 Tropes

| Dimension | ❌ 2015 Outdated Skinny / Fast Fashion |  2026 Blinc Architectural Luxury |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trouser Cut** | Low-rise, spray-on tapered thighs, tight ankles, bunching knee wrinkles | Ultra-high rise, deep twin inverted pleats, straight-to-wide leg pillar, fluid single puddle break over shoe |
| **Tailoring** | Short, skin-tight, waist-pinched X-creases, razor-thin lapels, stiff poly-blend | Extended seat coverage, unstructured floating canvas, soft drop shoulder, low double-breasted stance, broad peaked lapel |
| **Knitwear** | Thin clinging sweaters, tight armholes, cheap acrylic shine, rolled crew necks | Heavy boxy cropped slouch, 4" dropped shoulders, brushed mohair cloud halo, sculptural stand mock necks |
| **Outerwear** | Cluttered biker jackets (excess zips/straps), stiff short belted trenches | Brutalist minimal leather shells, floor-skimming ankle-length trenches, stripped of military clutter, oversized collars |
| **Textile Finish** | Shiny polyester satin, stiff synthetic wool, plasticky imitation leather | 380 GSM worsted Italian wool, 22mm sandwashed peachskin silk, full-grain French lambskin with satin wax patina |
| **Photography Style** | Over-saturated, blown-out ring light, studio white cyc, generic stock smile | Hasselblad medium-format, soft northern diffuse daylight, warm architectural limestone/concrete, contemplative poise |

---

## 2. Gemini Prompt Architecture & Technical Formula

To achieve museum-grade, hyper-realistic fashion lookbook photos with Gemini / Imagen 3, every prompt follows this standardized 8-part formula:

```
[Camera & Lens] + [Lighting & Environment] + [Subject & Poise] + [2026 Silhouette & Cut] + [Fabric Mechanics & Textures] + [Color Science & Undertones] + [Composition & Framing] + [Negative Guidance / Anti-Slop Directive]
```

### Universal Negative Directive (Append to every prompt or set in system):
> `No 2015 skinny jeans, no tight tapered trousers, no spray-on tailoring, no pulling X-wrinkles, no shiny synthetic fabric, no polyester sheen, no stock photography smiles, no airbrushed plastic skin, no CGI rendering, no deformed hands, no floating limbs, no brand logos or text overlays.`

## 6. How to Use These Prompts in the Gemini Chatbot

1. **Select Model:** In the Gemini chat interface, ensure you are using Gemini Advanced with **Imagen 3** enabled (or simply type your prompt directly; Gemini will route to Imagen 3 for image requests).
2. **Copy the Full Block:** Copy the entire prompt block for your desired garment and angle. Each prompt already includes the exact camera, lighting, styling, fabric details, and negative exclusions.
3. **Generate & Select:** Run 2–4 generations per garment to select the frame with the cleanest anatomical hands and most natural cloth drape.
4. **Export Specs:** Save the generated output at high resolution (PNG/JPEG) and replace the corresponding image placeholder in `src/data/products.ts` or `public/images/products/`.
